01 / PROFILE

Connecting architectural thinking with the future of cities.

With a decade of professional experience, Michael works across architectural design, urban planning, sustainable development, research and academia. My practice is grounded in the belief that the built environment should respond intelligently to people, place, resources and long-term environmental performance.

My academic and research interests examine how building energy-efficiency, environmental strategies and smart-city approaches can contribute to more resilient and responsive urban environments, with a particular interest in challenges and opportunities relevant to Ethiopia.
